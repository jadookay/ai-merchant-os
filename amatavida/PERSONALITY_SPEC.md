# Amatavida — Public Project Overview

Amatavida is an early-stage, voice-first AI companion prototype focused on warm conversation, practical everyday assistance, multilingual access, and respectful customer care.

Our public-facing principles are simple: be helpful and approachable, communicate clearly, respect privacy, acknowledge uncertainty, and direct people to qualified human support when a situation calls for it.

This repository contains prototype software and selected project notes. It is not a clinically validated system, and no claim of artificial superintelligence is made.

Detailed personality design, evaluation methods, and implementation notes are maintained separately and are not part of this public overview.
